## Açıklama (Description) — 2254/4000 karakter

```text
You talk, PopSub writes.

PopSub is a video editor for iPhone that turns the speech in your videos into Turkish captions automatically. Speech recognition tuned for Turkish catches every word and handles the timing for you, so you can stop typing captions by hand.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Pick your video: import a vertical video from Photos or Files. Trim it, speed it up, combine clips.
2. Automatic captions: one tap transcribes the speech word by word and times it for you. Fixing a wrong word takes seconds.
3. Pick a style and share: choose a ready-made style and export in 1080p, ready for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

FEATURES
• Automatic Turkish captions, word by word, with automatic timing
• Word-highlight styles: the spoken word lights up the moment it's said
• Ready-made styles: Clean, Bold outline, Background box and Word highlight
• Apply a style to the whole video with one tap
• Edit on your phone: trim, speed up, combine; captions stay in the right place as you edit
• Built for vertical 9:16 video with 1080p export
• Quick sign-in with your Apple account or email

YOUR VIDEO STAYS WITH YOU
Your videos and projects stay on your device. To create captions, only the audio is sent to our server, and the audio file is deleted within a few minutes after the job is done. We don't sell your data, we don't track you for advertising, and we don't use your recordings to train our own AI models.

LANGUAGE
PopSub is currently optimized for Turkish speech.

FREE AND PRO
• Free: 5 minutes of captions every calendar month. Unused minutes don't roll over to the next month.
• Pro: monthly and yearly subscriptions with 200 minutes of captions per month.
• Extra minutes: 20-minute packs available as in-app purchases.
Prices are shown in the App Store in your currency.

Subscriptions are billed to your Apple Account and renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Account → Delete account.

Privacy Policy: https://popsubapp.com/en/privacy/
Terms of Use: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
Support: https://popsubapp.com/en/support/
```
