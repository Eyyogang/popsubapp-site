Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Which languages does it support?

PopSub automatically turns Turkish, English and German speech into captions. For Turkish, there are extra improvements for proper names.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Your videos and projects stay on your device. To create captions, only the audio of your video is sent to our server. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

How does free usage work?

You get 5 minutes of free captions every calendar month; unused free minutes don’t roll over to the next month. With Pro, you get 200 minutes of captions every month (on the yearly plan, too, it’s 200 minutes every month). If you need more, you can also buy a one-time 20-minute pack.

When is it coming out?

PopSub is currently in testing. Write to us for early access and be the first to know when it’s ready.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Account → Delete account to permanently delete your account and your data on our server.
